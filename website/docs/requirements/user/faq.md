# FAQ User Requirements

## User Need

Users must get direct answers to high-intent questions, objections, and adoption
risks.

## Required Experience

The FAQ must answer questions plainly. It must avoid over-selling and must
state unsupported behavior or limits clearly.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The FAQ answers what Flavor Grenade LSP is.
- The FAQ explains whether it is an Obsidian plugin.
- The FAQ explains how it differs from Marksman.
- The FAQ explains whether Obsidian must be installed.
- The FAQ explains whether the tool edits the vault automatically.
- The FAQ explains which Markdown and OFM features are understood.
- The FAQ covers embeds, block references, tags, callouts, math, comments,
  frontmatter, and Templater regions.
- The FAQ explains whether Neovim or other LSP clients can use it.
- The FAQ explains how rename avoids unsafe edits.
- The FAQ explains why some links are not resolved.
- The FAQ explains how to report a bug with a minimal vault.
- The FAQ is eligible for FAQPage JSON-LD when implemented.
